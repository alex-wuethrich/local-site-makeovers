The Story

Made gently, by hand

Yelyzaveta started pouring candles at her kitchen table, blending soft floral scents until they felt just right — warm, quiet, never overwhelming. Every candle is still made in small batches, with natural soy wax, cotton wicks, and gentle, skin-safe fragrance oils.

No rush, no shortcuts — just quiet care, poured slowly and tested by hand before it ever reaches you.

The Process

From bloom to flame

01

Blend

Soft floral oils are measured and blended by hand, gently, until the scent feels just right.

02

Pour

Soy wax is warmed and poured slowly, with care, for a clean and even glow.

03

Cure

Each candle rests quietly for two weeks before it's trimmed, wrapped, and sent off.
