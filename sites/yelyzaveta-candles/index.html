The Story

Made by hand, on purpose

Yelyzaveta started pouring candles at her kitchen table, testing scent blends until they felt right — not too sweet, not too sharp. Every candle is still poured in small batches, using natural soy wax, cotton wicks, and phthalate-free fragrance oils.

No mass production, no shortcuts — just careful batches, tested by hand and burned before they're ever sold.
